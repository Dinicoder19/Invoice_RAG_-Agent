ACCOUNTS PAYABLE POLICY - MATCHABLE DATASET v1

Classification precedence: Duplicate-Suspected Invoice, Exception Invoice, Credit Note, Utility Invoice, Recurring Invoice, PO Invoice, Non-PO Invoice.

Duplicate-Suspected Invoice: duplicate_flag = Y. Route to Human Validation.
Exception Invoice: mandatory_fields_missing_flag = Y, supplier_valid_flag = N, or within_tolerance_flag = N for a PO invoice. Route to Human Validation.
Credit Note: document_type = Credit Note or invoice_amount < 0. Route to Credit Note Processing.
Utility Invoice: utility_flag = Y. Route to Utility Cost-Centre Owner validation.
Recurring Invoice: recurring_flag = Y, duplicate_flag = N, and mandatory_fields_missing_flag = N. Route to Subscription or Contract Owner validation.
PO Invoice: po_number is present, po_valid_flag = Y, vendor_match_flag = Y, within_tolerance_flag = Y, and goods_receipt_flag = Y.
Non-PO Invoice: po_number is blank, expense_permitted_without_po_flag = Y, utility_flag = N, recurring_flag = N, duplicate_flag = N, and mandatory_fields_missing_flag = N.

Approval matrix:
- Amount <= 1000: Department Manager approval.
- Amount > 1000 and <= 5000: Department Head approval.
- Amount > 5000: Finance Controller approval.
- Credit notes, duplicates, and exceptions use their special routes.

Confidence routing: confidence_score >= 0.90 is eligible for the next automated AP workflow step; below 0.90 routes to Human Validation.

EMERGENCY NON-PO INVOICE POLICY

Emergency non-PO invoices are permitted only when an urgent business,
facility, safety, operational-continuity, or critical-service requirement
prevents the normal purchase-order process.

An emergency non-PO invoice must include:
- a clear emergency justification,
- the requester name,
- the responsible cost center,
- invoice amount and vendor details.

Emergency non-PO invoices require Cost Center Manager approval before
Accounts Payable processing.

If requester, cost center, vendor, amount, or emergency justification is
missing, route the invoice to AP Exception Review / Human Validation.

PO MATCH EVIDENCE POLICY

For PO invoices, PO validity, vendor matching, tolerance validation,
goods-receipt confirmation, and supplier verification must be available
before automated AP processing.

If one or more required PO-match controls are missing, unknown, or not
available from ERP, PO, vendor-master, or goods-receipt data, route the
invoice to the AP Validation Queue.

Do not treat an UNKNOWN control as a failed control. UNKNOWN means that
additional validation evidence is required.
